The Frequency Almanac, by Vayelion Soul Maps
Effective date: July 16, 2026

The Frequency Almanac was built so that the most personal things you bring to it never leave your hands. This page says plainly what stays on your phone, what does not, and why.

What stays on your phone

Your birth name and birth date, if you choose to enter them, are stored only on your device and are used only to calculate your numerology reading there. They are never transmitted, never uploaded, and never seen by us or by anyone else. Your settings, your chosen room, and your notification preferences also live only on your device. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

What the app does not do

The Frequency Almanac has no accounts and no login. It shows no ads. It does not use analytics trackers, does not collect your location, contacts, photos, or browsing activity, and does not sell or share personal information with anyone.

Subscriptions

If you subscribe to Premium, the purchase is processed by Apple's App Store or Google Play, and our subscription service (RevenueCat) processes your purchase state together with a randomly generated app identifier so that your subscription works and can be restored. This identifier is not linked to your name or identity and is not used for advertising or tracking. Payment details are handled entirely by Apple or Google; we never see them.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily notification, it is scheduled locally on your device. No notification service outside your phone is involved.

Children

The Frequency Almanac is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. A change will never quietly reduce the protections described here.
