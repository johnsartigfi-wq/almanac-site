September 30, 2026: The First Lantern

Light Carried Into the Dark ✦ The Lamplighter ✦ Sending ✦ October's Threshold ✦ Kept Flame

A lantern in a window is nobody's accident: someone trimmed the wick before dark, filled the oil, kept the flame while no one watched. That is tending, light carried rather than awaited. The lamp burns for the road, not the room, so a traveler knows the house is awake. Whatever light you are keeping today, there is a window it could hang in.

Overall Frequency

In the cities of the old world, as the days shortened toward this very season, a figure appeared at dusk with a pole and a flame: the lamplighter, walking the rounds, leaving a trail of small lights behind him: and the children, the memoirs all say, watched for him: the dark not falling on the street, but the light, lamp by lamp, walking through it. This morning, September's last, with the equal night passed and the dark half fairly begun, the almanac closes its third month at that lamplighter's hour: and the traditions gather for a sending, as they gathered at July's horizon and August's turning light: but September's sending has its own instrument: not the horizon's road nor the season's gold, but the lantern: because the months ahead are the dark ones, and the lenses' unanimous counsel at their threshold is the day's name: do not enter the dark season hoping the lamps are lit: enter it as the lamplighter: the flame kept, the oil stocked, the light carried, and set, deliberately, where others can see by it.

A Reflection Question

What one flame am I committing to keep lit through the coming dark months?

A Practice for Today

Light a candle at dusk tonight. Make it the first of the season, and light it on purpose.
