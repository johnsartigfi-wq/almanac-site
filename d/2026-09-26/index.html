September 26, 2026: The Unwalked Road

Paths Not Taken ✦ Regret Transformed ✦ Peace with the Past ✦ The Unlived Lives ✦ Acceptance

Near three in the morning the road you never walked comes visiting, always sunlit, always open. That is regret, a road toured only in perfect weather, which is why it always wins. The road underfoot has potholes and slow traffic and the rare distinction of existing. There is room to mourn the bright one once. The commute this morning runs past actual trees.

Overall Frequency

At a certain hour, often around three in the morning or in a quiet September, the unwalked roads come visiting: the career not chosen, the person not married, the city not moved to, the child not had or the one had sooner, the risk declined, the yes withheld: the ghost-lives, running in parallel somewhere, forever unfalsifiably better than this one. Every adult carries a private map of them. This morning the traditions gather around that map, because regret is among the most universal and least discussed of the heart's weathers: and their counsel is more interesting than either of the culture's two slogans: not "no regrets," which is denial wearing sunglasses: and not the ghost-roads' permanent residency, which is a life spent commuting to nowhere: but a third way: the unwalked roads mourned honestly, mined for their information, and then, decisively, released: the traveler returned, whole, to the one road that has the unique distinction of existing.

A Reflection Question

Which life I did not choose visits me at three in the morning, showing only its good days?

A Practice for Today

Write down one life you did not choose, its best days and its worst ones both. Seen whole in daylight, it loses much of its power.

Universal Guidance

✦

The ghost-roads are toured only at their sunniest, which is why they always win the 3 a.m. comparison. Mourn them once, honestly, take their one instruction, and come back: the road underfoot is the only one with weather, and it is also the only one still going anywhere.
