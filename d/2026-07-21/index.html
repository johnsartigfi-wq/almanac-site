July 21, 2026: The Even Scale

Justice ✦ Fairness ✦ Integrity ✦ Right Dealing ✦ Responsibility

A cashier counts change back across the counter, coin by coin. The cashier can see into the drawer; the customer cannot. It is easiest to count short when the other side cannot look in. Fairness is the full count, given when no one is watching. Tonight you might ask whether your own day comes out even.

Overall Frequency

Among the oldest surviving human artifacts are weights: small polished stones, calibrated, carried by traders across the bronze-age world. Among the oldest surviving human laws are curses on those who filed them down. This morning the traditions gather around the scale, the most universal moral instrument ever built, and around its quiet daily question, which has never changed in four thousand years: when you weigh, do you weigh even? When you deal, does the other side of the table get the truth?

A Reflection Question

Where I hold the power, would the other side of the table call my scale even?

A Practice for Today

Pay one thing you have been slow to pay: the invoice, the loan, the favor, the compliment someone earned. Before the day ends.
