Overall Frequency

The first cold morning sends everyone to the closet, and the closet tells the truth. The coat that served last winter hangs where it was left in March: a button loose, a seam opening at the shoulder, the lining parting at the pocket where keys go. None of this failed dramatically. It wore, the way covering things wear, thread by thread, while nobody was cold enough to look. October's third day looks now, on purpose, while the weather is still forgiving and the mending is still small.

A Reflection Question

Which of my coverings have I not inspected since the last time I needed it?

A Practice for Today

Bring out the winter coat today: check buttons, seams, lining, and zipper, and repair or arrange repair before the first truly cold day.
